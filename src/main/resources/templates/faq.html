Category 3 — Pricing

The current standard RideSmart rental plan is ₹500 for 12 hours and includes up to 80 KM of distance allowance.

Additional distance beyond the included 80 KM is charged at ₹3 per additional KM.

Additional rental time beyond the scheduled return time is charged at ₹50 per additional hour according to the applicable rental calculation.
