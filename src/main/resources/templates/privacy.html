Privacy Policy

How RideSmart collects, uses, protects, and handles customer identity data.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

RideSmart is committed to respecting customer privacy and protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for users of the RideSmart bike rental platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to fulfill rental requests, verify rider identity, maintain vehicle security, and process billing transactions.

2. Account Information

During account creation, we collect your full name, email address, 10-digit mobile number, date of birth, residential address, and emergency contact details.

3. KYC & Identity Documents

To satisfy vehicle verification requirements, customers submit copies of their Aadhaar Card, valid Driving License, and Selfie identity proof. Documents are stored in encrypted directories outside the public web root and served exclusively via protected endpoints.

4. Rental & Booking Information

We record booking references, vehicle selection, pickup/return datetimes, odometer readings, pre/post inspection notes, and generated rental agreements.

5. Payment Information

We record payment methods (Cash, UPI), payment statuses (`PENDING`, `PAID`), total transaction amounts, and UPI UTR reference numbers. RideSmart does not store bank account passwords or credit card CVVs.

6. GPS & Location Data

Where GPS telemetry is enabled, vehicle position coordinates, speed, and cumulative distance are recorded during active rental trips for fleet management and theft protection.

7. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to operate rental workflows, confirm customer eligibility, generate legal contracts, calculate extra distance charges, and issue tax invoices.

8. Data Security & Encryption

We employ BCrypt password hashing, role-gated Spring Security authorization filters, and secure file streaming to protect customer records against unauthorized access.

9. Data Sharing & Disclosure

RideSmart does not sell, rent, or trade customer personal data to third-party marketers. Data may only be disclosed when required by Indian statutory authorities or law enforcement.

10. Data Retention Policy

Customer identity documents and booking logs are retained for the duration of the account lifecycle to support audit reconciliation and tax compliance.

11. User Rights & Access

Customers have the right to view their submitted documents, active bookings, rental history, invoices, and digital agreements at any time via the customer dashboard.

12. Account & Data Deletion

Customers may request account deactivation or document deletion upon completion of all active rentals and settlement of outstanding dues by contacting support.

13. Cookies & Session Management

We use essential HTTP session cookies solely to maintain authenticated user login sessions and prevent cross-site request forgery.

14. Policy Updates

RideSmart may update this policy periodically to reflect platform enhancements or regulatory changes. Updated versions will display the effective date on this page.

15. Contact Us

If you have privacy concerns or data access requests, please contact our support team via official support channels listed on the Contact page.
