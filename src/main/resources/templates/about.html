Step-by-Step Rental Journey

Everything you need to know from online bike selection to vehicle return and final invoice settlement.

STEP 01

1. Choose Your Bike

Browse the available fleet and select the bike that best suits your needs. Compare vehicle models, fuel type, engine capacity, included distance allowance, and live availability.

Browse Fleet
STEP 02

2. Select Your Rental Period

Choose your pickup date, pickup time, and expected return time. Standard rental duration is 12 Hours with 80 KM distance included at a base rate of ₹500.

STEP 03

3. Complete KYC Verification

Provide the required identity and verification documents before the rental is activated. Required proofs include: Aadhaar, Valid Driving Licence, Selfie, and Address Proof. Verification is required before vehicle handover.

STEP 04

4. Confirm Your Booking

Review the selected vehicle, rental duration, included distance (80 KM), and applicable charges. Confirm your reservation and access your Booking ID details.

STEP 05

5. Pick Up Your Bike

Complete the vehicle handover and inspection process. Record relevant vehicle condition, starting odometer reading, and fuel information before starting the rental.

STEP 06

6. Ride Responsibly

RideSmart vehicles may use GPS-enabled tracking to support vehicle security, operational monitoring and rental management in accordance with applicable policies.

STEP 07

7. Return the Bike

Return the vehicle at the agreed location and time. The vehicle is inspected and the final rental amount calculated based on applicable rental rules (Extra KM @ ₹3/KM, Overdue @ ₹50/hr).

About RideSmart

RideSmart is a digital bike rental management platform designed to simplify the complete rental journey — from discovering an available vehicle and completing customer verification to booking, inspection, active rental monitoring, payment and final settlement.

Our platform brings the major operational parts of two-wheeler rental into one structured workflow, helping customers understand their booking while helping rental operators manage vehicles, documents, rentals, payments and operational records with complete accountability.
