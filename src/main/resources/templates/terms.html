1. Eligibility

Customers must meet the following eligibility requirements before requesting or receiving a rental vehicle:

  • Meet the minimum legal age applicable to the rental (minimum 18 years).
  • Hold a valid, original Indian Driving Licence appropriate for the rented two-wheeler category.
  • Provide accurate registration, contact, and identity information.
  • Complete required customer KYC verification prior to rental activation.
  • Provide genuine and valid identity documents.
  • Meet RideSmart's booking requirements.
2. Customer Information & KYC

Required identity verification documents currently specified before vehicle handover:

Aadhaar
Driving Licence
Selfie Proof
Address Proof

Documents are used strictly for identity and eligibility verification and are stored in encrypted directories.

3. Booking Conditions

A booking request is subject to vehicle availability, customer identity verification, and payment confirmation. A booking is confirmed when recorded with active status in the RideSmart system.

4. Rental Duration

The customer must return the vehicle by the agreed return date and time. Rental duration is calculated using pickup and expected return timestamps recorded at booking.

5. Pricing

Current RideSmart standard rental pricing parameters:

• Base Rental Plan: ₹500.00 for 12 Hours
• Included Distance Limit: 80 KM
• Additional Distance Rate: ₹3.00 per KM
• Additional Rental Time Rate: ₹50.00 per Hour

Example: A 12-hour rental costs ₹500 base price with up to 80 KM included. If the customer travels 10 KM beyond the included distance, 10 × ₹3 = ₹30 additional distance charge is added.

6. Included Distance

The standard 12-hour rental plan includes up to 80 KM allowance. Odometer starting and ending readings recorded during pickup and return inspection determine actual distance traveled.

7. Additional Distance

Distance traveled beyond the included 80 KM limit is charged at ₹3.00 per additional KM upon vehicle return settlement.

8. Late Return Policy

If the vehicle is returned after the agreed return time, additional rental duration is charged at ₹50.00 per additional hour according to configured settlement rules.

9. Vehicle Condition

Vehicle condition and odometer readings are recorded during pickup and return. Customers are expected to return the vehicle in substantially the same condition received, subject to normal wear.

10. Prohibited Use

RideSmart vehicles must not be used for:

  • Illegal activities or transporting illegal substances.
  • Reckless driving, speed racing, or stunt riding.
  • Allowing unauthorized persons to operate the vehicle.
  • Tampering with GPS or vehicle security hardware.
  • Sub-renting or transferring the vehicle to third parties.
11. Driving & Safety

Customers are responsible for operating the vehicle safely, wearing mandatory helmets, and complying with statutory speed limits (max 80 km/h).

12. Fuel Handling

Fuel status is recorded at pickup. Hirers agree to return the vehicle with equivalent fuel levels.

13. GPS Monitoring

RideSmart vehicles may be equipped with GPS tracking for vehicle security, fleet management, operational support and rental administration in accordance with applicable policies.

14. Supported Payments

Current supported payment methods are Cash and UPI. For UPI payments, unique transaction UTR reference numbers are recorded for reconciliation.

15. Cancellation Policy

Cancellation eligibility is determined according to the cancellation policy applicable to the booking and displayed during reservation.

16. Customer Responsibilities

Customers must provide genuine identity documents, operate the vehicle safely, return on time, and report issues promptly.

17. RideSmart Responsibilities

RideSmart is committed to providing roadworthy two-wheelers, maintaining transparent digital records, and protecting user identity data.

18. Accidents, Damage & Breakdown

In the event of an accident or breakdown, hirers must immediately notify RideSmart customer support and follow emergency safety protocols.

19. Privacy & Data Handling

Personal information and identity proofs uploaded during KYC are handled in accordance with the RideSmart Privacy Policy.

20. Amendments & Acceptance

RideSmart reserves the right to amend these terms. Continued platform usage constitutes acceptance of published updates.

21. Contact & Support

For queries regarding these terms or rental bookings, reach out via the official RideSmart Support page.
